TCP Reachability Probe (real browser-issued connections)

Idle.
Browsers are sandboxed and cannot open raw TCP sockets, so this uses real fetch() connections and measures the outcome: a fast TypeError means the connection was refused (port closed), a successful opaque response means the port answered, and a timeout means the packet was dropped (filtered). Two caveats are unavoidable: HTTPS pages cannot open http:// connections (mixed content), and Private Network Access blocks requests from public pages to private IPs. When that happens the result is reported as blocked-by-browser, not as a fake result.
